## What is covered

The content of the website https://hamzasabek.github.io/statscool/ and of this repository that is
meant to teach: the texts and explanations, the quiz questions and their explanations, the recap
sheets, the guided-tour texts, the English and French wording, and the design of the games (what
the student does, what is measured, how the graphs present it). In the repository these live mostly
in the `text.js`, `*Text.js`, `quiz.js` and `recapText.js` files under `src/`.
